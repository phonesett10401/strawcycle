Questions people ask

Honest, general answers. Our products are a class concept, so none has a certificate yet.

Is it food-safe?

Straw-fibre packaging is generally made food-safe with a suitable coating. Ours would be tested to Thai FDA rules before real sale.

Is it microwave-safe?

Plain straw-fibre packaging generally tolerates short, gentle reheating, but coatings and lids vary. We would test it and print the answer on each product, and until then we would not recommend it.

Does it compost?

Straw fibre is plant material and generally breaks down, but how fast depends on any coating and on whether it goes to a proper composting site. Ask any producer for a test result before relying on a claim.
